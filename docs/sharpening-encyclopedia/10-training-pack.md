# Sharpening Training Pack

## Important Scope Note

This is a **knowledge curriculum outline and comprehension check**, not a real accredited certification and not a substitute for supervised, hands-on practice. Sharpening is a physical craft skill — reading a burr by feel, maintaining a consistent freehand angle, judging heat by touch, operating grinding equipment safely — that genuinely cannot be transferred by a written document alone, any more than reading about knife skills makes someone a trained cook. Nothing here should be presented to a customer, employee, or anyone else as "Xtra Sharp certified" or an equivalent claim of formal accreditation — this project has no authority to issue a real trade certification, and doing so would misrepresent what completing this material actually means. Treat it as a structured reading list and self-check, paired with real supervised practice for anyone actually learning to sharpen.

## Knowledge-Check Questions

Comprehension checks against the material above — not a certification exam. Answers are findable in the referenced documents; working through them without looking is a reasonable self-test of retention.

**Beginner:**
1. What's the difference between honing and sharpening?
2. Why does water-cooled sharpening protect a blade's temper?
3. What is a burr, and why does leaving it in place cause an edge to dull quickly after sharpening?
4. Name the three item categories that use water-cooled sharpening.
5. Name the two item categories that primarily use machine-guided sharpening, and explain why their process differs from water-cooled sharpening.

**Intermediate:**
6. What four factors does machine-guided scissors sharpening address together, beyond the edge itself?
7. Why does a wide clipper blade suit different jobs than an A5 blade?
8. Why do garden tools typically use a wider edge angle than kitchen knives?
9. What's the difference between surface rust staining and genuine pitting, and why does the distinction matter for restoration work?
10. Why is reinstallation of processor blades outside a sharpening service's scope?

**Advanced:**
11. What causes edge angle to "drift" over years of freehand home sharpening, and how is it corrected?
12. Explain the relationship between steel hardness and toughness, and why it matters for edge angle selection.
13. What's the difference between martensite and the structure produced by tempering, at a basic level?
14. When is it appropriate to tell a customer their item isn't worth restoring, and how should that be communicated?
15. What distinguishes routine quality control from a genuine restoration-level assessment at intake?

**Professional:**
16. What factors go into setting an initial sharpening interval for a new business client, and how should that interval be adjusted over time?
17. Walk through the correct response, step by step, to a customer reporting a damaged item found at mail-in intake.
18. What's the difference between the business's existing (live) policies and the drafted policy frameworks, and why does that distinction matter?
19. When should a review request be sent, and why does timing matter?
20. Describe the emergency procedure for a lost return-postage parcel, including who is responsible for lodging the claim.
