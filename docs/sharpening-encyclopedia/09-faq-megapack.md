2. **Do I need to book in advance for mail-in too?** Contact first to confirm pricing and satchel size before posting anything.
3. **What payment methods are accepted?** Cash, card, or internet banking.
4. **Can I get a quote without committing to anything?** Yes — call, text, or send a photo for condition-dependent items.
5. **Is there a minimum order size?** No — a single small item is fine.

9. **Can businesses set up a recurring arrangement?** Yes — see `docs/long-form-content/12-salon-sharpening-schedule.md` and `15-commercial-kitchen-blade-maintenance-scheduling.md`.

## Knives (12)

11. **How often should I sharpen my kitchen knives?** See `docs/marketing-pack/blog-posts/01-how-often-sharpen-kitchen-knives.md`.
12. **What's the difference between honing and sharpening?** Honing realigns; sharpening restores. See `08-glossary.md`.
13. **Will sharpening change my knife's shape?** A proper sharpen restores the edge, not the overall shape.
14. **Can a rusty old knife be saved?** Often yes — depends on rust depth; see `docs/long-form-content/11-restoring-a-neglected-knife.md`.
15. **Do you sharpen serrated knives?** Yes, with a technique that preserves the tooth pattern.
16. **What's water-cooled sharpening?** See `docs/technical-pack.md`.
17. **Why did my home-sharpened knife's angle change over the years?** Freehand sharpening drift — see `06-science-and-theory.md`.
18. **What cutting board should I use?** Wood or plastic — glass/stone/ceramic dulls blades faster.
19. **Is a sharper knife more dangerous?** No — less force needed means less slip risk.
20. **What steel is my knife made of?** Usually stainless or carbon — see `docs/long-form-content/09-steel-types-and-sharpening.md`.
21. **Can you fix a chipped tip?** Often yes, depending on severity — see `docs/troubleshooting-pack/knives.md`.
22. **How do I store knives properly?** See `docs/customer-education-pack/storage.md`.
