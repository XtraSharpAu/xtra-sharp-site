# Operational Templates

Short, channel-agnostic templates (work as email or SMS) for the specific transactional moments in a job's lifecycle. For longer-form marketing email versions of some of these, see `docs/marketing-pack/email-templates.md` — these are the tighter, operational versions for day-to-day use.

## 1. Booking Confirmation

> Hi [Name], confirming your booking: [item(s)] for [drop-off on DATE / mail-in]. Quoted at $[price]. [If drop-off: address sent closer to the time.] [If mail-in: pack per the instructions and let me know once posted.] — Phil, Xtra Sharp

## 2. Drop-Off Confirmation

> Hi [Name], your drop-off is confirmed for [date/time] at [address]. See you then — call or text 0412 974 277 if anything changes.

## 3. Mail-In Instructions

> Hi [Name], here's what to do: wrap each item separately, protect the tips, pack tightly, include a prepaid satchel (or let me know to add postage to your total), and post to [address]. Full guidance: xtrasharp.com.au/mail-in

## 4. "Items Ready for Pickup"

> Hi [Name], [item(s)] are sharpened and ready for pickup. Come by any time during our arranged window — thanks!

## 5. "Items Shipped"

> Hi [Name], your sharpened [item(s)] are on the way back — tracking number: [XXXXX]. Should arrive within the usual Australia Post window.

## 6. "Items Arrived" (mail-in intake confirmation)

> Hi [Name], your parcel arrived safely and everything's checked over. Getting started now — expect it back within 1–2 business days.

## 7. "Items Delayed"

> Hi [Name], quick update — [item(s)] will take a bit longer than expected, due to [honest reason]. New expected time: [X]. Sorry for the delay, and thanks for your patience.

## 8. "Damage Found"

> Hi [Name], wanted to flag before I start — [item] arrived with [specific damage/condition]. Can you confirm how you'd like me to proceed? Happy to talk it through — 0412 974 277.

## 9. "Additional Cost Required"

> Hi [Name], after a closer look, [item] needs [reason — e.g. more extensive restoration than a standard sharpen] which brings the total to $[revised price] instead of the original $[quoted price]. Let me know if you'd like to go ahead, or if you'd rather discuss first.

## 10. "Thank You for Your Business"

> Hi [Name], thanks for choosing Xtra Sharp for your [item(s)]. Hope it's cutting exactly how you'd want. If you've got a spare minute, a Google review helps other locals find us: [link]. Appreciate your business — Phil.
