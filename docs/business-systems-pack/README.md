# Business Systems Pack

The full internal operations manual for Xtra Sharp — customer service scripts, workflows, checklists, policies, templates, training material, growth systems, and emergency procedures.

This replaces the earlier single `docs/business-systems-pack.md` file (its 9 workflows are preserved and expanded into `02-workflow-documentation.md`).

## Grounding

Everything here is either drawn directly from what's already live and established (the `/terms` page's real policy language, the master pricing table, the site's boilerplate copy) or clearly marked as a **draft requiring the business owner's decision** where it touches a genuine commercial or legal commitment. Two things surfaced while building this pack that are worth knowing up front:

1. **Turnaround time is stated inconsistently on the live site.** `/terms` says "Standard turnaround is 24–48 hours." The newer canonical service pages (rebuilt more recently) say "same day or next day" for drop-off and "1–2 business days after arrival" for mail-in. These aren't necessarily incompatible (24–48 hours roughly covers "next day"), but they're not the same claim, and `04-business-policies.md` surfaces this rather than silently picking one to write into new policy documents.
2. **There's no existing refund or warranty policy anywhere on the site.** `/terms` covers appointments, item condition, repairs/damage, turnaround, pricing/payment, pick-up/mail-in, transit loss, safety/liability, and cancellations — but says nothing about refunds or warranty. `04-business-policies.md` drafts a framework for both with the actual terms left as explicit decisions for the business owner, not invented.
