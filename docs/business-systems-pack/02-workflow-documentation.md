# Workflow Documentation

Draft SOPs for the business owner to confirm against actual practice. The five item-specific sharpening workflows build on the generic 10-step process in `docs/technical-pack.md` (intake → assessment → method selection → restoration → edge-forming → burr removal → tension/alignment [scissors/clippers only] → cleaning → QC → return) by adding what's genuinely different per item type, rather than repeating the generic steps five times.

## 1. Drop-Off Sharpening Workflow

1. Customer makes contact (call/text/form) to enquire or request a quote.
2. Pricing and rough scope confirmed (photo requested if condition-dependent).
3. Appointment time agreed.
4. Full address (Woodbine, Campbelltown NSW) sent ahead of the appointment.
5. Customer arrives; items briefly inspected together at hand-off so pre-existing condition is noted with the customer present.
6. Sharpening carried out per the relevant item-specific workflow below.
7. Customer notified when ready (typically same day/next day — see `04-business-policies.md`'s note on the turnaround wording inconsistency).
8. Item collected, payment taken.
9. Follow-up and review request per workflows 11–12 below.

## 2. Mail-In Sharpening Workflow

1. Customer makes contact before posting — pricing, satchel size, and turnaround confirmed.
2. Customer packs and posts items (per `docs/customer-education-pack/prepare-items.md`), including a prepaid return satchel or agreeing to postage-on-invoice.
3. Parcel arrives, logged against the booking.
4. Contents checked for transit damage and matched against what was expected (see `01-customer-service-scripts.md` Section 8 if damage is found).
5. Sharpening carried out per the relevant item-specific workflow below.
6. Items repackaged securely (see `03-checklists.md`'s Packaging & Shipping checklist).
7. Posted back via Australia Post with tracking; tracking number sent to the customer.
8. Payment reconciled per the method agreed at step 1.
9. Follow-up and review request per workflows 11–12 below.

## 3. Knife Sharpening Workflow

Builds on the generic sharpening workflow with: assessment specifically checks for chips, tip damage, and angle drift from prior home sharpening; water-cooled equipment used throughout edge-forming to protect temper (see `03-checklists.md`'s Temper Protection checklist); final test is a real cut test (paper or soft food), not just visual inspection.

## 4. Scissors Sharpening Workflow

Builds on the generic workflow with an explicit tension/alignment pass as a distinct step, not assumed to happen automatically during edge-forming. Bevel vs. convex is confirmed at intake (ask the customer if not obviously identifiable) since the technique differs materially — see `docs/long-form-content/02-scissors-sharpening.md`.

## 5. Clipper Blade Sharpening Workflow

Each blade is sharpened individually (not batch-processed as a group without individual attention), then cleaned, aligned, and test-run on the actual clipper body (or a compatible test unit) before being marked complete — test-fit and function check is the distinguishing extra step for this category.

## 6. Garden Tool Sharpening Workflow

Assessment includes a handle/pivot check alongside the edge (a cracked axe handle or loose secateur pivot is a separate issue from blade sharpness — see `docs/troubleshooting-pack/garden-tools.md`). Water-cooled throughout. Sap/resin residue is cleaned off before grinding, not just after, since built-up residue can affect grinding consistency.

## 7. Processor Blade Sharpening Workflow

Power-down and safe-handling procedures apply throughout (see `docs/long-form-content/05-processor-blade-safety.md`). Reinstallation is explicitly out of scope of this workflow — the blade is returned to the customer or their technician for reinstallation per their own equipment's manufacturer guidance, not reinstalled by Xtra Sharp unless that's a specifically agreed additional service.

## 8. Quality Control Workflow

See `docs/technical-pack.md`'s full QC Checklist, applied to every item before return. Where practical for a single-operator business, a deliberate second look at a different time (not immediately after finishing the grind) catches more than checking in the same moment as finishing the work.

## 9. Cleaning and Sanitising Workflow

- **Items**: cleaned of sharpening residue (swarf, grinding debris) before packaging or hand-back — a distinct step, not assumed automatic.
- **Between clients/items** (particularly clipper blades and grooming-adjacent items): wipe down and sanitise contact surfaces between different customers' items, standard hygiene practice for any service handling multiple people's equipment.
- **Equipment**: grinding surfaces and water reservoirs (for water-cooled equipment) cleaned/maintained on a regular schedule — see `03-checklists.md`'s Machine Maintenance checklist for specifics.

## 10. Packaging for Return Postage Workflow

1. Item cleaned and fully dry.
2. Edge/tip protected (cardboard, cloth, or a dedicated guard).
3. Wrapped individually if multiple items are returned together.
4. Packed into the return satchel/box provided, or an appropriate replacement if the customer's supplied packaging isn't suitable (flagged to the customer if so).
5. Tracking number generated and recorded before dispatch, then sent to the customer.

## 11. Customer Follow-Up Workflow

1. **At hand-back/dispatch**: confirm the customer knows the item is ready/sent, and how to reach out if anything's not right.
2. **A few days later** (informal check-in, not automated for every job unless volume supports it): "How's it cutting?" — see `docs/marketing-pack/email-templates.md`/`sms-templates.md` for ready-to-use versions.
3. **If a complaint or issue arises**: handled per `01-customer-service-scripts.md` Section 6, tracked through to resolution, not left open-ended.

## 12. Review Request Workflow

1. Sent only after a completed, successful job — never bundled into an unresolved complaint or delay.
2. Direct link to the GBP review page (once the listing exists — see `docs/seo-pack.md` Section 15) to minimise friction.
3. Asked once per job, not repeatedly chased — see `07-business-growth-systems.md`'s Review Acquisition System for the full approach and the policy against incentivised reviews.
4. Every review — positive or negative — gets a response (see `07-business-growth-systems.md`).
