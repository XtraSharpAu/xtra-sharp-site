# Checklists

## 1. Knife Inspection Checklist

- [ ] Item type and expected condition matches what was booked/quoted
- [ ] Edge condition assessed (even wear vs. chips vs. rolled edge)
- [ ] Tip checked for damage
- [ ] Handle/tang checked for looseness (separate issue from sharpening — flag, don't attempt to fix unless asked)
- [ ] Rust or corrosion noted
- [ ] Existing angle assessed against expected angle for that knife type (`docs/technical-pack.md`'s angle reference table)
- [ ] Any pre-existing damage photographed and noted before work begins

## 2. Scissors Inspection Checklist

- [ ] Bevel or convex edge confirmed
- [ ] Tension checked (too loose/too tight/correct)
- [ ] Ride line/alignment visually checked
- [ ] Pivot condition checked (stiff, loose, or correct)
- [ ] Edge condition assessed
- [ ] Any damage to tips or handles noted

## 3. Clipper Blade Inspection Checklist

- [ ] Blade type confirmed (A5, wide, large-animal)
- [ ] Top and bottom blade alignment checked
- [ ] Edge condition on both blades assessed
- [ ] Nicks or chips noted
- [ ] Mounting/hub condition checked for fit issues unrelated to sharpening

## 4. Garden Tool Inspection Checklist

- [ ] Tool type confirmed (secateurs/hedge shears/axe/tomahawk)
- [ ] Pivot bolt condition checked (secateurs/shears)
- [ ] Handle condition checked (axes/tomahawks) — cracks or looseness flagged as a safety issue, separate from sharpening
- [ ] Edge condition assessed
- [ ] Rust/sap residue noted

## 5. Processor Blade Inspection Checklist

- [ ] Blade type and size confirmed (home/industrial)
- [ ] Edge condition assessed
- [ ] Mounting hub/shaft fit checked visually (not adjusted — reinstallation is the customer's/their technician's responsibility, see `02-workflow-documentation.md`'s Processor Blade workflow)
- [ ] Damage or wear beyond normal dulling noted and flagged before proceeding

## 6. Temper Protection Checklist

- [ ] Water-cooled equipment used for knives, garden tools, and processor blades throughout grinding
- [ ] No visible heat discolouration (straw/blue tint) present after sharpening
- [ ] Grinding pace matched to cooling capacity — not rushed in a way that risks heat build-up
- [ ] Blade checked for temperature by touch periodically during longer jobs (a genuinely hot blade mid-process indicates heat management needs adjusting)

## 7. Machine Maintenance Checklist

- [ ] Grinding surfaces cleaned of swarf/residue on a regular schedule
- [ ] Water reservoir (for water-cooled equipment) checked and refreshed regularly, not left stagnant
- [ ] Wear on grinding surfaces monitored — replaced per manufacturer guidance, not run past their effective life
- [ ] General equipment condition checked per the equipment's own manufacturer maintenance schedule (this checklist doesn't substitute for that documentation)

## 8. Safety Procedures Checklist

- [ ] Every item handled by spine/non-cutting section during intake, inspection, and packaging
- [ ] Processor blades: power disconnected before any handling (see `docs/long-form-content/05-processor-blade-safety.md`)
- [ ] Damaged/structurally compromised items flagged and customer contacted before proceeding, not sharpened regardless
- [ ] Sharp, completed items stored/transported edge-protected, never loose

## 9. Customer Communication Checklist

- [ ] Pricing confirmed before work begins (from the master table, or a photo-quote for condition-dependent items)
- [ ] Turnaround expectation given clearly and honestly (see `04-business-policies.md`'s turnaround wording note)
- [ ] Any pre-existing damage communicated before proceeding
- [ ] Customer notified when item is ready/dispatched
- [ ] Tracking number sent for mail-in returns
- [ ] Follow-up sent per `02-workflow-documentation.md` Section 11

## 10. Packaging and Shipping Checklist

- [ ] Item cleaned and fully dry before packing
- [ ] Edge/tip protected
- [ ] Wrapped individually if multiple items in one parcel
- [ ] Packed tightly enough that contents can't shift in transit
- [ ] Return address/contact details included inside the parcel, not just on the external label
- [ ] Tracking number generated and recorded before dispatch

## 11. Drop-Off Intake Checklist

- [ ] Customer identity/booking matched
- [ ] Items match what was booked (type and quantity)
- [ ] Condition briefly checked together with the customer present
- [ ] Any discrepancy from the original quote flagged immediately, not after the customer has left
- [ ] Expected pickup time confirmed before the customer leaves

## 12. Mail-In Intake Checklist

- [ ] Parcel matches an existing booking/contact record
- [ ] Contents checked against what was expected
- [ ] Condition assessed and photographed if anything unexpected is found
- [ ] Customer contacted if there's any discrepancy, damage, or missing return postage arrangement
- [ ] Logged as received before starting any work
