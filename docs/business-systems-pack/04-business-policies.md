# Business Policies

## Important: What's Real vs. Draft

Six of these nine policies already exist, live, on `/terms` — those are reproduced/summarised below as the **actual current policy**, not redrafted. Three do not currently exist anywhere on the site (**Warranty**, **Refund**, and a standalone **Communication** policy) — those are drafted as frameworks with the real commitment left as an explicit decision, not invented. A policy like a refund or warranty policy is a real financial and legal commitment to customers — publishing one with terms nobody actually decided on creates genuine risk if a customer later holds the business to wording that was never actually approved.

## 1. Drop-Off Policy *(existing — from `/terms`)*

All services are appointment-only; walk-ins are not accepted. A drop-off/pick-up/mail-in arrangement must be confirmed before sending or delivering items. Local pick-up is available in selected Western Sydney areas, quoted based on distance.

## 2. Mail-In Policy *(existing — from `/terms`, expanded with site-wide mail-in copy)*

Mail-in customers are responsible for postage to Xtra Sharp. Return postage can be included in the quote or billed separately. **Xtra Sharp is not responsible for items lost or damaged during postage to us** — customers are advised to package items securely and consider tracked shipping for that outbound leg. (Note: the *return* leg, once Xtra Sharp posts items back, is always sent with tracking — this is a separate, existing commitment stated on the newer mail-in-focused pages and in `docs/business-systems-pack/02-workflow-documentation.md`'s Packaging workflow. The two legs have different arrangements and that distinction should stay clear in any customer-facing wording.)

## 3. Warranty Policy *(does not currently exist — draft framework)*

No warranty policy is currently published anywhere on the site. A reasonable framework, with the actual terms left for the business owner to decide:

- **What might be covered**: if a sharpening job itself was faulty (e.g. the edge wasn't actually properly formed, burr wasn't removed, tension/alignment was wrong on scissors/clippers) within `[X days]` of the job, a free re-sharpen could be offered.
- **What would not be covered**: normal dulling from subsequent use, damage occurring after the item left Xtra Sharp's care, or issues unrelated to the sharpening itself (e.g. a pre-existing cracked handle).
- **Decision needed**: the actual time window (`[X days]`), and whether "free re-sharpen" is the full remedy or whether refunds are ever offered under warranty (see Refund Policy below — these two policies should be decided together, since they overlap).

## 4. Safety Policy *(existing — from `/terms`, expanded)*

"Sharpened items are extremely sharp. Customers are responsible for safe handling after collection or delivery. Xtra Sharp is not liable for injuries caused by misuse or improper handling of sharpened tools." Internally, this is paired with the business's own safe-handling practices during the sharpening process itself — see `03-checklists.md`'s Safety Procedures checklist and `docs/long-form-content/05-processor-blade-safety.md`.

## 5. Customer Care Policy *(synthesised from established site-wide commitments, not from a single existing policy page)*

Drawn from commitments already made consistently across the live site's copy, not invented for this document:

- Contact first before proceeding on anything unusual — items are checked on arrival and the customer is contacted before work proceeds if something needs extra attention or isn't suitable for sharpening.
- Transparent, upfront pricing — published rates, with photo-quotes offered for condition-dependent items rather than surprise pricing after the fact.
- Honest turnaround communication — see the Turnaround Time Policy below for the one open inconsistency to resolve.

## 6. Refund Policy *(does not currently exist — draft framework)*

No refund policy is currently published. A reasonable framework, again with real terms left as an explicit decision:

- **If a job was clearly faulty** (see Warranty Policy above) and a re-sharpen isn't practical or hasn't resolved it, is a partial or full refund offered? `[decision needed]`
- **If a customer changes their mind before work has started**, is a full refund/no-charge the default? (This seems like a reasonable, low-risk default, since no work or cost has been incurred — but should still be an explicit, stated decision rather than assumed.)
- **If work has already been completed correctly** but the customer is unhappy for reasons unrelated to sharpening quality (e.g. they decide they didn't need it done), is that refundable? `[decision needed — many service businesses reasonably do not refund correctly-completed work]`

## 7. Damage Policy *(existing — from `/terms`, this is the most complete existing policy)*

"Minor chips, bends, and tip repairs are included where possible. Severe damage is quoted individually. If an item is unsafe or structurally compromised, sharpening may be refused." Combined with the transit-damage position in the Mail-In Policy above (not Xtra Sharp's responsibility for outbound transit damage) and the safety-liability position (not liable for post-handover misuse). For damage occurring *during* Xtra Sharp's own process specifically — not currently addressed anywhere, including on `/terms` — see `01-customer-service-scripts.md` Section 8 for how this should be handled conversationally; the actual resolution (repair contribution, replacement, refund) is a case-by-case business decision, not something to pre-script a guaranteed outcome for.

## 8. Turnaround Time Policy *(existing, but inconsistently worded — needs reconciling)*

`/terms` states: "Standard turnaround is 24–48 hours. Large batches or specialty tools may take longer. Urgent jobs are available on request and quoted based on workload." The newer canonical service pages state "same day or next day" for drop-off and "1–2 business days after arrival" for mail-in. These aren't necessarily contradictory in substance (24–48 hours roughly overlaps with "next day" / "1–2 business days"), but they're worded as if they're different claims, which risks a customer holding the business to whichever version they read. **Recommendation: pick one consistent form of words and update every page that states a turnaround claim to match** — this is a genuine content-consistency fix, not a new policy decision, and is also flagged in `docs/website-audit-pack.md`.

## 9. Communication Policy *(does not currently exist as a standalone policy — synthesised from established practice)*

- Contact first before proceeding on anything unusual (see Customer Care Policy above).
- Proactive updates on delays (see `01-customer-service-scripts.md` Section 7) rather than waiting to be chased.
- All quotes for condition-dependent items confirmed before work begins, not after.
- Preferred contact channels: phone, SMS, or the site's contact form (which opens the visitor's email client — see `docs/maintenance.md` item #1 for the known limitation there).
