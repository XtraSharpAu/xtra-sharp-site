# SMS Templates (10)

Short, appropriate for actual SMS length. `[Name]`/`[item]`/`[price]`/`[date]` are placeholders.

1. **Enquiry reply**: Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to Xtra Sharp. I'll confirm pricing and turnaround shortly — or call me directly on 0412 974 277.
2. **Quote given**: Hi [Name], your [item] sharpening quote is $[price]. Drop-off (Campbelltown, by appointment) or mail-in both work — let me know how you'd like to proceed.
3. **Appointment confirmation**: Hi [Name], your drop-off is confirmed for [date]. Full address to follow. Text 0412 974 277 if you need to change anything.
4. **Reminder day-before**: Hi [Name], just a reminder your sharpening drop-off is tomorrow at [time]. See you then!
5. **Mail-in received**: Hi [Name], your parcel has arrived safely and sharpening is underway. Should be back with you within 1–2 business days.
6. **Ready for pick-up**: Hi [Name], your items are sharpened and ready for pick-up. Thanks for choosing Xtra Sharp!
7. **Posted back**: Hi [Name], your sharpened items have been posted back with tracking — keep an eye out for the satchel.
8. **Review request**: Hi [Name], hope your [item] is cutting great! A quick Google review would really help — [link]. Thanks again!
9. **Satchel size confirm**: Hi [Name], for [item] a [small/medium/large] Australia Post satchel should be perfect. Let me know if you have any questions before posting.
10. **General booking CTA**: Sharp knives, scissors, clipper blades, garden tools & processor blades — drop-off in Campbelltown or mail-in Australia-wide. Call/text 0412 974 277 to book.
