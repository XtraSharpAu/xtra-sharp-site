# Google Business Profile Posts — Seasonal, Round 2 (5)

Five more ready-to-publish GBP posts, covering themes from `seasonal-campaign-concepts.md` that weren't used in `google-business-posts-seasonal.md` (round 1: Spring, Pre-Summer, New Year, Back to School, Autumn, Winter, End of Financial Year, Wedding/Salon Season, Pre-Christmas Hospitality, Season Change). This round picks 5 of the remaining themes from that same 20-theme list. No discount or dollar figures invented — see `promotional-offer-concepts.md` for why.

1. **[Seasonal — Christmas/Holiday Cooking]** Hosting over the holidays? Get your kitchen knives sharpened before the cooking starts, not after the roast is half-carved with a blunt blade. *CTA: Book Now*

2. **[Seasonal — Gift Idea]** Looking for a practical gift for the cook, gardener or groomer in your life? Book their knives, secateurs or scissors in for a sharpen — a gift they'll actually use. *CTA: Message*

3. **[Seasonal — Off-Season Storage]** Whatever's going into the shed or drawer for the season — secateurs, hedge shears, even a knife you won't touch for a while — sharpen it first. A stored blade should come back out ready to work, not rusty and dull. *CTA: Call Now*

4. **[Seasonal — School Term Start]** New school term, new craft and home ec supplies — a reminder that scissors get free pick-up and drop-off for local schools. *CTA: Learn More*

5. **[Seasonal — New Financial Year]** New financial year — a good time for commercial clients to set up a recurring sharpening cycle for the year ahead instead of booking one-off jobs as needed. *CTA: Message*
