# Email & SMS Templates — Round 2 (20 More Each)

Additive to `email-templates.md` and `sms-templates.md`. Condensed format for this round — subject/message only.

## Email Templates (20)

1. **Seasonal — Garden Tools (Spring)**: "Time to sharpen the secateurs?" — reminder to get garden tools sharpened before the growing season.
2. **Seasonal — Knives (Christmas/Holiday Cooking)**: "Sharp knives for the holiday cooking season" — timely reminder ahead of a busy cooking period.
3. **New Service Announcement**: "We now sharpen garden tools and processor blades" — for past customers who may not know the full current service range.
4. **Batch/Commercial Follow-Up**: "Following up on batch sharpening for [business]" — after an initial commercial enquiry.
5. **Satisfaction Check-In**: "How's your [item] cutting?" — a few weeks after a job, informal check-in.
6. **Referral Ask**: "Know someone who needs their knives sharpened?" — light-touch referral request to happy past customers.
7. **Price List Update Notice**: "Updated pricing at Xtra Sharp" — sent only if/when pricing genuinely changes, linking to current `/pricing`.
8. **Missed Appointment Follow-Up**: "Let's reschedule your drop-off" — friendly follow-up if a booked appointment was missed.
9. **Parcel Delay Notice**: "Your parcel is taking a little longer than expected" — proactive update if mail-in transit is delayed.
10. **Damage Found on Arrival**: "A quick note about your [item]" — used when an item arrives with pre-existing damage needing discussion before work proceeds.
11. **First-Time Customer Welcome**: "Welcome to Xtra Sharp" — sent after a first booking, setting expectations for the process.
12. **Business Client Onboarding**: "Setting up your sharpening schedule" — for a new commercial/batch client agreeing to a recurring cycle.
13. **End-of-Financial-Year Reminder (Commercial)**: "Wrapping up the year — need your equipment serviced?" — timely nudge for business clients.
14. **Bad Weather / Postal Delay Notice**: "Postal delays may affect your mail-in turnaround" — proactive, honest communication during known Australia Post disruptions.
15. **Long-Time-No-See Re-Engagement**: "It's been a while!" — for customers who haven't booked in 12+ months.
16. **Feedback Request (Neutral, Not Just Reviews)**: "How did we do?" — genuine feedback request, distinct from a public review ask.
17. **Holiday Closure Notice**: "A quick note about our availability over [period]" — for any planned downtime.
18. **New Suburb Coverage Announcement**: "Now servicing [suburb]" — if service area expands.
19. **Thank You After Large/Batch Job**: "Thanks for the big job!" — acknowledgement after a larger-than-usual order.
20. **Apology/Service Recovery**: "Sorry we didn't get this right" — for use if something genuinely goes wrong, owning it directly.
