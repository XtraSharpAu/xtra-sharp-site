# FAQ Expansions (10)

Marketing/landing-page-oriented Q&As, distinct from the extra FAQs already drafted in `docs/content-expansion-pack.md` (which focuses on gaps in the live `/faq` page itself). These are framed for use in ads, landing pages, or GBP Q&A seeding.

1. **"How much does knife sharpening cost?"** — From $6 for a small paring knife, up to $15 for large knives. Full pricing at xtrasharp.com.au/pricing.

2. **"Do you offer mobile or on-site sharpening?"** — No — sharpening is drop-off only from our dedicated area in Woodbine, Campbelltown NSW, or mail-in Australia-wide. This keeps every job to the same consistent, accurate standard.

3. **"How do I know what my item will cost before committing?"** — Send a photo and we'll confirm an exact quote before you drop off or post anything.

4. **"Is mail-in sharpening safe for expensive knives/scissors?"** — Yes, provided items are packed following our guidance (wrap individually, protect tips, use a sturdy box) — see the full instructions at xtrasharp.com.au/mail-in.

5. **"Can I get multiple different items sharpened at once?"** — Yes — knives, scissors, clipper blades, garden tools and processor blades can all be brought or sent together in one visit.

6. **"Do you sharpen for businesses, not just individuals?"** — Yes — schools, salons, restaurants and workshops can arrange batch pricing and scheduled sharpening cycles.

7. **"What areas do you service for drop-off?"** — Campbelltown, Woodbine and the wider Macarthur region (Camden, Narellan, Gregory Hills, Oran Park, Harrington Park, Mount Annan and surrounding suburbs).

8. **"What if I'm not local — can I still use Xtra Sharp?"** — Yes — mail-in sharpening is available Australia-wide with tracked return postage.

9. **"How long has Xtra Sharp been operating?"** — 13+ years of sharpening experience, Mirror Cut® accredited, and a Flamin' Sharp licensee.

10. **"What payment methods do you accept?"** — Cash, card, and internet banking.
