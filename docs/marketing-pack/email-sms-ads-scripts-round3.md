2. **Salon Schedule Proposal**: "A sharpening schedule for [salon name]" — following up after discussing a regular schedule, with a proposed interval and next steps.
3. **Commercial Maintenance Schedule Proposal**: "Processor blade maintenance plan for [business]" — same concept for a commercial kitchen client.
4. **Blade Selection Advice**: "Which clipper blade for the job?" — informational follow-up for a groomer who asked about blade selection.
5. **Post-Restoration Care Advice**: "Keeping [item] in good shape after restoration" — care tips sent after a significant restoration job, so the investment holds up.
