# Seasonal Campaign Concepts (20)

Campaign **themes and timing**, tied to real seasonal relevance already established in this pack — not specific discount terms (see `promotional-offer-concepts.md` for why offer numbers aren't invented here). Each can be paired with an offer concept from that file if a promotional angle is wanted, or run purely as a content/awareness campaign.

1. **Spring Garden Tool Prep** (Sep–Oct) — secateurs, hedge shears, axes; ties to `docs/long-form-content/04-garden-tool-care.md`.
2. **Pre-Summer Knife Check** (Nov) — kitchen knives ahead of holiday entertaining season.
3. **Christmas/Holiday Cooking Season** (Dec) — knife sharpening push ahead of peak home-cooking period.
4. **New Year Kitchen Refresh** (Jan) — "start the year with sharp tools" angle.
5. **Back-to-School Scissors** (late Jan/early Feb) — school and craft scissors.
6. **Autumn Garden Wind-Down** (Mar–Apr) — final garden tool sharpen before tools go into off-season storage.
7. **Mother's/Father's Day Gift Angle** — sharpening as a practical, unusual gift idea for a keen home cook.
8. **Winter Grooming Push** (Jun–Jul) — clipper blade sharpening ahead of a season groomers often report as busy for coat maintenance.
9. **End of Financial Year Business Push** (Jun) — commercial/batch clients servicing equipment before year-end.
10. **Hunting Season Prep** (timing dependent on local hunting seasons) — hunting knife sharpening.
11. **Wedding Season Salon Push** (Sep–Dec, coinciding with peak wedding season) — hairdressing scissors for salons with high seasonal demand.
12. **Restaurant Pre-Christmas Push** (Nov) — processor blades and knives for hospitality businesses ahead of their busiest period.
13. **National Tradies Day / Trade-Focused Awareness** (if applicable locally) — axes, garden tools, workshop-adjacent items.
14. **Autumn Pruning Campaign** (Mar–May, depending on plant types) — secateurs and hedge shears.
15. **"Sharpen Before You Store" Off-Season Campaign** — encouraging customers to sharpen tools before seasonal storage, so they're ready (not blunt) when the season returns.
16. **Local School Term Start** — reinforcing the free school pick-up/drop-off offer at each term's start.
17. **Community Event Tie-In** — awareness campaign timed around any local Campbelltown/Macarthur community event Xtra Sharp participates in or sponsors.
18. **Small Business Month Push** (if observed locally) — B2B/commercial angle.
19. **"New Financial Year, New Sharpening Schedule"** (Jul) — commercial clients setting up recurring cycles.
20. **General "Season Change" Reminder** (any solstice/equinox) — simple, recurring "time to check your tools" awareness post, not tied to a specific category.

## Notes

- Timing above is based on general Australian seasonal patterns (Southern Hemisphere) — confirm against the business owner's actual observed busy/quiet periods before locking in a calendar, since real demand patterns may differ from these general assumptions.
- None of these require a discount to run — several work purely as content/awareness campaigns using material already in this pack (blog posts, social posts, guides).
