# Customer Scripts (10)

Phone/in-person conversation guides. Adapt tone naturally in conversation — these are structure prompts, not word-for-word scripts to read aloud.

## 1. Incoming Call — General Enquiry

> "Xtra Sharp, this is Phil, how can I help?"
> [Listen to what they need sharpened]
> "Sure, I can sort that out. Can you tell me roughly the condition, and how many items? ... Okay, that'll be around $[price] — could vary a little depending on condition, but happy to confirm from a photo if you want to send one first. Would you prefer to drop off here in Woodbine, or post it in?"

## 2. Booking a Drop-Off Appointment

> "Great, let's get you booked in. What day/time works for you? ... I'll text you the address closer to the time — it's a dedicated sharpening area in Woodbine, listed publicly as Campbelltown NSW. Anything else you'd like sharpened while you're in?"

## 3. Explaining Mail-In for the First Time

> "No problem, mail-in works really well. Wrap each item separately — tea towels or bubble wrap are fine — and make sure the blade tips are well protected so they can't punch through the box. Pop in a prepaid satchel for the return, or I can add postage to your total. Once it arrives I'll check it over and you'll have it back within 1–2 business days."

## 4. Quoting an Uncertain/Damaged Item

> "That one's a bit hard to quote without seeing it — condition makes a real difference on [hunting knives/axes/processor blades]. If you can send me a photo I'll give you an exact price before you commit to anything."

## 5. Upselling a Second Service (Scissors Customer Also Has Knives)

> "While you're getting the scissors done, did you want me to take a look at any knives too? Saves a second trip, and I can usually turn both around together."

## 6. Commercial/Batch Client First Contact

> "For a salon/school/restaurant your size, it usually makes sense to set up a regular sharpening cycle rather than one-off jobs — keeps everything consistent and you're never caught out with blunt gear. Want me to put together batch pricing for your setup?"

## 7. Handling a Turnaround-Time Question

> "Most drop-off jobs are same day or next day, depending on how many items and what they are. Mail-in usually adds 1–2 business days once it arrives, plus postage time each way."

## 8. Handling a Price-Objection / "That Seems Expensive"

> "Totally understand — happy to walk you through it. [Convex scissors/large processor blades] take more time and a more precise process than a standard item, which is reflected in the price. If it helps, I'm always upfront about pricing before you commit — no surprises at pick-up."

## 9. Post-Job Pick-Up / Hand-Back

> "All done — here you go. [Point out the edge/finish if relevant.] If you run into anything, just give me a call. And if you've got a minute, a quick Google review really helps other locals find us."

## 10. Declining a Job (Item Not Suitable / Out of Scope)

> "I don't want to take your money for something I can't do justice to — [reason, e.g. this one's too far gone to safely restore / this isn't something I sharpen]. Happy to point you in the right direction if I know someone who can help."
