# Brochure Variations (10)

Copy specs for tri-fold (6-panel) brochures — more detailed than the flyers, structured as Panel 1 (front cover) → Panel 6 (back cover). For a designer to lay out; not finished print files.

## 1. General Services Brochure

- **Front cover**: "Xtra Sharp — Professional Knife & Tool Sharpening" / tagline: "Sharp Tools. Sharp Results." / phone number
- **Inside left**: "Our Services" — one paragraph each for Knives, Scissors, Clipper Blades, Garden Tools, Processor Blades
- **Inside centre**: Full pricing table (all 5 categories, matching `docs/schema.md`'s master table)
- **Inside right**: "How It Works" — Drop-off (Woodbine, Campbelltown NSW, by appointment) and Mail-In (Australia-wide, prepaid satchel, 1–2 business day turnaround)
- **Back panel**: Contact details, service area suburb list, "13+ years experience, Mirror Cut® accredited, Flamin' Sharp licensee"

## 2. Knife Sharpening Brochure

- **Front**: "Knife Sharpening — Water-Cooled Precision"
- **Inside**: Explanation of water-cooled sharpening (heat damage protection), full knife pricing table, knife types served (kitchen, butcher, large, hunting)
- **Back**: Drop-off/mail-in options, CTA

## 3. Scissors Sharpening Brochure (Salon/Barber-Targeted)

- **Front**: "Scissors Sharpening for Professionals"
- **Inside**: Bevel vs. convex explainer, machine-guided process description, full scissors pricing
- **Back**: "Ask about regular sharpening schedules for salons and barbershops"

## 4. Clipper Blade Brochure (Groomer/Vet-Targeted)

- **Front**: "Clipper Blade Sharpening for Groomers & Clinics"
- **Inside**: A5/wide/large-animal blade pricing, individual-blade-testing process description
- **Back**: Batch sharpening note for multi-clipper businesses

## 5. Garden Tools Brochure

- **Front**: "Garden Tools Sharpening"
- **Inside**: Water-cooled process, secateurs/hedge shears/axes pricing, "why a sharp blade makes a healthier cut" explainer
- **Back**: Seasonal reminder framing (spring garden prep)

## 6. Processor Blades Brochure (Commercial-Targeted)

- **Front**: "Processor Blade Sharpening — Home & Commercial"
- **Inside**: Home vs. industrial blade pricing, "keeps your equipment running efficiently" framing
- **Back**: B2B contact CTA for restaurants, butchers, food processors

## 7. Mail-In Sharpening Brochure

- **Front**: "Mail-In Sharpening — Australia Wide"
- **Inside**: Full 12-step how-it-works process (condensed from `/mail-in`), satchel size guidance, pricing reference
- **Back**: Turnaround time, tracked return postage explanation

## 8. Commercial/Business Clients Brochure

- **Front**: "Sharpening Solutions for Your Business"
- **Inside**: Batch pricing framing, scheduled sharpening cycles, target industries (schools, salons, restaurants, workshops)
- **Back**: Case-for-outsourcing framing — consistent edge quality vs. in-house sharpening

## 9. Service Area / Local Coverage Brochure

- **Front**: "Your Local Sharpening Service — Campbelltown & Macarthur"
- **Inside**: Full suburb list, drop-off explanation ("why drop-off only"), mail-in as the wider-reach option
- **Back**: Contact + map reference

## 10. New Customer Welcome Brochure

- **Front**: "Welcome to Xtra Sharp"
- **Inside**: Full service + pricing overview, "13+ years experience" trust section, what to expect on first visit
- **Back**: FAQ highlights (turnaround, packing instructions, payment methods), contact details
