# Sharpening Appointment Etiquette — What to Do (and Avoid) Before You Arrive

A few simple things before a drop-off or mail-in appointment make the process smoother for everyone — none of them complicated, all of them easy to overlook.

## Do: Clean Items Before Drop-Off

Wiping off food residue, hair, dirt, or oil before an appointment isn't just courtesy — a clean item is easier to inspect properly before sharpening, so any wear, damage, or alignment issue is easier to spot and flag before work begins.

## Do: Label Items If Bringing Multiple Pieces

If you're dropping off more than one item — several knives, a full set of clipper blades, a mixed batch for a household or a business — labelling helps make sure everything is tracked correctly and returned to the right owner, especially for batch or commercial drop-offs.

## Do: Detach Clipper Blades From the Clipper Body

Clipper blades should come off the clipper body before drop-off or posting — this is standard practice for sharpening and keeps the clipper body itself out of the process entirely.

## Do: Confirm Your Appointment Before Arriving

Drop-off is appointment-only — turning up without a confirmed time isn't something the current setup accommodates, since sharpening runs from a dedicated appointment-based area rather than a walk-in shopfront. Confirming ahead of time avoids a wasted trip.

## Avoid: Sending Items in a Loose Bag Without Protection

Loose blades — especially multiple sharp items together — risk damage to each other and are simply less safe to handle in transit or on arrival. A secure container, individual wrapping, or padded packaging protects both the item and whoever's handling it.

## Avoid: Assuming Every Item Needs the Same Turnaround

Most items are completed within 1–2 days, but busy periods can extend that, and anything genuinely urgent should be flagged at booking rather than assumed. If timing matters for a specific job, say so upfront rather than after drop-off.

## Avoid: Skipping the Photo for an Unusual Item

If an item doesn't clearly fit a standard category, or condition might affect pricing, sending a photo ahead of drop-off means a quote can be confirmed before anything changes hands — no surprises either way.

## The Bottom Line

None of this is complicated — clean items, labelled if there are several, appointment confirmed, packed securely, and anything unusual flagged ahead of time. Getting these small things right before your appointment means less back-and-forth and a smoother job all round.
