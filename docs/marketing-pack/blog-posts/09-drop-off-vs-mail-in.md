# Drop-Off vs. Mail-In: Which Sharpening Option Is Right for You?

Xtra Sharp offers two ways to get your items sharpened — drop-off, and mail-in. Neither is "better" outright; the right choice depends on where you are and what suits your schedule.

## Drop-Off Sharpening

**Best for**: anyone in Campbelltown, Woodbine, or the wider Macarthur region (Camden, Narellan, Gregory Hills, Oran Park, Harrington Park, Mount Annan and surrounding suburbs).

**How it works**: drop-off sharpening is by appointment only, from a dedicated sharpening area in Woodbine (listed publicly as Campbelltown NSW) — the full address is provided once your appointment is confirmed. Most drop-off jobs are turned around same day or next day, depending on the number of items and what they are.

**Why it suits some people better**: if you're local, drop-off means no postage cost, no waiting for parcels to travel each way, and the fastest possible turnaround. It's also the simplest option if you want to talk through a quote or condition issue face-to-face before committing.

## Mail-In Sharpening

**Best for**: anyone outside the local drop-off area — mail-in sharpening is available Australia-wide.

**How it works**: pack your items securely (see our full packing guide), post them with a prepaid Australia Post satchel included for the return trip, and expect your sharpened items back within 1–2 business days of arrival, sent with tracking.

**Why it suits some people better**: distance is simply not a factor. Whether you're a few suburbs outside the drop-off area or on the other side of the country, mail-in sharpening gives you access to the exact same water-cooled and machine-guided processes used on drop-off items — there's no difference in sharpening quality between the two options, only in how the item gets to us and back.

## What's the Same Either Way

- **Pricing** is identical regardless of how the item arrives — see the full price list for every category.
- **Process** is identical — the same water-cooled sharpening for knives, garden tools and processor blades, and the same machine-guided sharpening for scissors and clipper blades, whether the item was handed over in person or arrived by post.
- **Condition checks** happen either way — items are checked on arrival (in person or by post), and if something needs extra attention or isn't suitable for sharpening, you'll be contacted before any work proceeds.

## What's Different

| | Drop-Off | Mail-In |
|---|---|---|
| Availability | Campbelltown/Macarthur region only | Australia-wide |
| Turnaround | Same day/next day typical | 1–2 business days after arrival, plus postage time each way |
| Cost | No postage | Postage cost (or add to final total) |
| Booking | By appointment | Contact first to confirm satchel size and pricing |

## Can You Mix the Two?

Yes — some customers drop off locally most of the time but use mail-in when travelling or if a batch of items needs to go in while they're away. There's no requirement to pick one method permanently; use whichever suits a given situation.

## The Bottom Line

If you're within reach of Campbelltown or the Macarthur region, drop-off will usually be the faster, simpler option. If you're anywhere else in Australia, mail-in gives you the exact same sharpening quality without needing to travel — just factor in the extra transit time each way.
