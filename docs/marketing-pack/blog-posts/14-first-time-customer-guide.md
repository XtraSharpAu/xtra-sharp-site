# What First-Time Customers Should Know Before Booking

Booking a sharpening service for the first time raises a few reasonable questions — here's what to expect, in one place.

## Drop-Off or Mail-In?

If you're in Campbelltown, Woodbine, or the wider Macarthur region, drop-off is straightforward — book an appointment, and you'll get the address closer to the time (drop-off is appointment-only, not walk-in). If you're further away, mail-in sharpening covers the rest of Australia with the same sharpening quality, just with postage time added on each end.

## Getting a Price Before You Commit

Every service category has published pricing, so you can check roughly what to expect before reaching out. For items where condition genuinely affects the price (a hunting knife with unknown wear, for instance), you can send a photo and get an exact quote before committing to anything — you're never charged a surprise amount after the fact.

## What Happens to Your Item

Every item is checked on arrival — whether handed over in person or received by post — and if anything unusual or concerning is found, you'll be contacted before any work proceeds, not after. See our "behind the scenes" article for the full process from arrival to hand-back.

## How Long It Takes

Drop-off jobs are typically same day or next day. Mail-in adds transit time on top of a 1–2 business day sharpening turnaround once your parcel arrives — so factor in postage time each way when planning around a specific date.

## Packing for Mail-In

If you're posting items in, there's a straightforward process: wrap items individually, protect the tips, pack tightly, include your contact details and a return satchel (or have postage added to your total). Full guidance is available before you need to post anything — contact first and everything will be confirmed, including the right satchel size.

## Payment

Cash, card, or internet banking are all accepted, whichever suits you.

## If You're Not Sure Your Item Is Worth Sharpening

This comes up more than you'd think — a genuinely old or neglected item where it's not obvious whether sharpening is worthwhile. The honest answer is always given upfront: if a full sharpen isn't going to produce a good result for what it would cost, that's communicated clearly rather than proceeding anyway.

## The Bottom Line

Booking for the first time doesn't need to be complicated: check the published pricing, choose drop-off or mail-in based on your location, and expect clear communication at every step — before work begins, if anything unusual comes up, and once your item's ready. If anything's unclear, a quick call or text sorts it out before you commit to anything.
