# Future Development Pack

Speculative ideas, not commitments or a roadmap. Each should be evaluated against real customer demand and business capacity before any of it gets built — nothing here is pre-approved.

## Ideas for New Pages

- A real `/blog` section publishing the articles already drafted in `docs/marketing-pack/blog-posts/` and `docs/long-form-content/` — currently these exist only as docs, not live pages.
- A `/gallery` rebuild (the existing one is a legacy page) showing real before/after photos once photography exists (`docs/image-handling.md`).
- A `/reviews` or testimonials page, once real reviews exist (never before — see `docs/seo-pack.md`'s review policy).
- A dedicated `/chisels` or `/trade-tools` page if that category grows enough to warrant its own page rather than a footnote elsewhere.
- Rebuild `/services`, `/commercial`, and `/batch` onto the current canonical section pattern (closing the structural/pricing gap flagged in `docs/website-audit-pack.md`).

## Ideas for New Services

- **Sharpening subscription** — a recurring plan for regular customers (e.g. quarterly knife sharpening) rather than one-off bookings.
- **Equipment inspection/assessment service** — a paid or free assessment for commercial clients unsure whether ageing equipment needs sharpening or replacement.
- **Sharpening classes/workshops** — teaching basic home maintenance (honing, storage, cutting board choice) as a small side offering, which also reinforces why professional sharpening matters.
- **Chisel and woodworking tool sharpening** as a formally listed category, if demand exists (chisels are already mentioned in the existing FAQ content but aren't a dedicated service page).

## Ideas for New Content

- Video content showing the actual sharpening process — genuinely differentiating once real footage/photography exists, and a natural fit for the water-cooled/machine-guided explainer content already written.
- A downloadable PDF version of the packing instructions, for customers who want a printable reference.
- Case studies for commercial clients (with permission) showing real before/after impact of a sharpening schedule.

## Ideas for New Marketing

- Local business cross-promotion with hairdressers, groomers, butchers, and restaurants who are also customers (see `docs/seo-pack.md` Section 7's backlink strategy).
- A referral program (see `docs/marketing-pack/promotional-offer-concepts.md` #2).
- Seasonal campaign calendar execution (see `docs/marketing-pack/seasonal-campaign-concepts.md`).
- Local directory listings (True Local, Yellow Pages, Localsearch — see `docs/seo-pack.md` Section 7).

## Ideas for New Automation

- Automated review request follow-up (email/SMS sent a set number of days after job completion) — currently a manual process per `docs/business-systems-pack.md`'s workflows.
- A real backend for the contact form (see `docs/maintenance.md` item #1) so submissions don't depend on the visitor's own email client.
- Simple booking/calendar tool for drop-off appointments, reducing back-and-forth on scheduling.
- Automated satchel-size suggestion tool on `/mail-in`, based on item type/quantity selected — though this would need real, honest logic behind it rather than a gimmick.

## Ideas for Customer Retention

- A loyalty mechanism (see `docs/marketing-pack/promotional-offer-concepts.md` #8).
- Scheduled reminder emails/SMS based on typical resharpening intervals per item type (see `docs/customer-education-pack/when-to-sharpen.md` for the signals that could inform timing).
- A simple customer record system (even a basic spreadsheet) tracking what each returning customer has had sharpened and when, to personalise re-engagement rather than generic blanket messages.

## Ideas for Business Growth

- Formalise commercial/batch relationships with salons, restaurants, schools and butchers into standing scheduled arrangements rather than ad hoc bookings (see `docs/business-systems-pack.md`).
- Consider a second drop-off day/time slot structure if demand grows beyond what's comfortably handled by appointment-only single-operator capacity.
- Evaluate expanding the service area suburb list as real demand data (enquiries from outside the current listed area) becomes available.
- Track which service categories generate the most enquiries vs. bookings, to prioritise future content/marketing investment (e.g. this pack's SEO keyword work in `docs/seo-pack.md`) toward what's actually converting.
