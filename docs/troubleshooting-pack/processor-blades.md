# Processor Blade Troubleshooting

> **Scope**: this covers blade condition and cutting performance only. For anything involving the equipment itself — motor, electrics, installation, or general machine operation — consult your equipment's manufacturer documentation or a qualified technician. See `docs/long-form-content/05-processor-blade-safety.md` for the same boundary.

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Uneven or torn product instead of a clean cut | Dull or damaged blade | Book a sharpen |
| Motor sounds like it's working harder than usual | Blade resistance increasing due to dullness, forcing the motor to compensate | Sharpen the blade rather than continuing to run the equipment under increased strain — and note this is itself a safety consideration, not just a performance one |
| Visible nicks or unevenness on the blade edge | Impact damage, or normal wear reaching the point of needing attention | Book a sharpen; mention any known damage when booking |
| Inconsistent results across a batch | Blade dulling partway through a run, or uneven wear along the blade | Consider a more frequent sharpening schedule if this happens regularly in commercial use |
| Blade doesn't seat correctly after reinstallation | Fitting issue specific to your equipment, not a sharpening issue | Consult your equipment's manufacturer documentation for correct installation |
| Rust or discolouration on the blade | Moisture left on the blade without full drying | Wash and dry thoroughly after each use, before storing |
| Uncertain whether a large industrial blade is safe to keep using | Genuine wear/damage assessment needed | Contact us for an assessment before continued use — we'll advise honestly if sharpening can restore it or if replacement should be considered |

For anything beyond blade condition — unusual noise from the motor, electrical issues, or equipment not operating as expected — this is outside what a sharpening service can advise on; your equipment's manufacturer or a qualified technician is the right resource.
