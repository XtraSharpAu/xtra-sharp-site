# Scissors Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Scissors feel "loose" or floppy | Tension has drifted — a sharpening issue as much as a mechanical one | Machine-guided sharpening addresses tension along with the edge |
| Pushing hair/fabric rather than cutting cleanly | Dull edge, or ride line misalignment | Book a sharpen — if it persists after sharpening, ride line may need further adjustment |
| Uneven results on what should be a straight cut | Micro-unevenness in the edge, or tension inconsistency along the blade length | A full sharpen restores both edge and tension consistently |
| Scissors stiff/hard to operate | Tension too tight, or pivot needs lubrication | Check pivot lubrication first; if still stiff, tension may need adjusting during sharpening |
| Audible change in cutting sound (duller/softer) | Common early sign of a dulling edge, often noticed before a visible difference | Worth booking a sharpen soon, before performance visibly drops |
| Increased hand fatigue during use | Compensating for reduced sharpness or incorrect tension by gripping harder | Sharpen and check tension rather than continuing to compensate |
| One blade appears to sit higher/lower than the other at the pivot | Alignment has shifted | This is addressed as part of machine-guided sharpening's alignment step |
| Scissors "chatter" or vibrate during a cut | Edge unevenness or ride line issue | Full sharpen with alignment check |
| Rust or discolouration near the pivot | Moisture/product residue not cleaned off, especially in grooming/salon environments | Wipe down after each use; dry thoroughly |
| Convex-edge scissors losing their smooth cutting feel | Convex edges wear differently to bevel and need the specific convex sharpening technique | Confirm with us that your scissors are convex when booking, so the correct technique is used |
| Thinning scissors not thinning evenly | Teeth unevenly worn, or standard sharpening technique applied instead of matching the thinning pattern | Mention it's a thinning pair specifically when booking |
| Scissors squeak during use | Pivot needs lubrication — not an edge issue | A drop of appropriate lubricant at the pivot; if it persists, the pivot mechanism itself may need attention beyond sharpening |
