# Clipper Blade Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Blade pulls/tugs instead of gliding | Dull edge, or misalignment between the two blades | Book a sharpen — machine-guided sharpening addresses both edge and alignment |
| Blade runs hot during use | Insufficient oiling, dull blade, or alignment issue increasing friction | Check oiling first; if it persists, the blade likely needs sharpening |
| Visible nicks along the edge | Impact damage from contact with hard debris or incorrect handling | Can usually be addressed during sharpening, depending on severity |
| Uneven cut / missed patches | Teeth not lining up correctly between the top and bottom blade | Check visual alignment before use; if consistently uneven, a professional sharpen is needed |
| Blade feels "grabby" on thicker coats | Blade may be due for sharpening, or tension between the blade pair needs adjustment | Book a sharpen — tension/tracking is addressed alongside the edge |
| Clippers louder or more strained-sounding than usual | Blade resistance increasing due to dullness, forcing the motor to work harder | Sharpen the blade rather than continuing to run the clipper under strain |
| Rust or discolouration on the blade | Moisture or hair/product residue not cleaned off after use | Clean and oil after every use |
| Blade doesn't seat correctly on the clipper body | Wear at the mounting point, or a mismatch between blade and clipper model | Check compatibility with your specific clipper model; this is separate from sharpening |
| Newly sharpened blade still pulling | Alignment may need a second look, or the blade wasn't given time to "settle" correctly on reinstallation | Contact us if a freshly sharpened blade isn't performing as expected — this shouldn't happen |
| Multiple blades in a kit performing inconsistently | Blades sharpened at different times/intervals rather than as a set | Consider a batch sharpening arrangement so the whole kit stays consistent |
