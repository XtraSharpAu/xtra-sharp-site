# Business Systems Pack

Internal operational workflows (SOPs). These are drafted from the process already described on the live site (`/mail-in`'s 12-step process, the established boilerplate in `docs/content-style-guide.md`) plus the sharpening-specific workflow in `docs/technical-pack.md` — treat as a draft SOP set for the business owner to confirm matches actual practice, not as documentation of a system that's been formally verified against real operations.

**Customer service scripts**: see `docs/marketing-pack/customer-scripts.md` — not duplicated here.

## Drop-Off Workflow

1. Customer makes contact (call/text/form) to enquire or request a quote.
2. Pricing and rough scope confirmed (photo requested if condition-dependent).
3. Appointment time agreed.
4. Full address (Woodbine, Campbelltown NSW) sent to the customer ahead of the appointment.
5. Customer arrives, items handed over — condition briefly checked together at hand-off so any pre-existing damage is noted with the customer present.
6. Sharpening carried out (see `docs/technical-pack.md`'s sharpening workflow).
7. Customer notified when ready (same day/next day typical).
8. Item collected, payment taken (cash, card, or internet banking).
9. Review request sent as a follow-up (see `docs/marketing-pack/email-templates.md` / `sms-templates.md`).

## Mail-In Workflow

1. Customer makes contact before posting anything — pricing, satchel size, and turnaround confirmed.
2. Customer packs and posts items (per `docs/customer-education-pack/prepare-items.md`), including a prepaid return satchel or agreeing to have postage added to the final total.
3. Parcel arrives — logged against the customer's booking.
4. Contents checked for transit damage and matched against what was expected.
5. Sharpening carried out (see `docs/technical-pack.md`'s sharpening workflow).
6. Items repackaged securely for return.
7. Posted back via Australia Post with tracking; tracking details sent to the customer.
8. Payment reconciled (prepaid, invoiced, or added to postage-inclusive total, as agreed at step 1).
9. Review request sent as a follow-up.

## Quality Control Workflow

See `docs/technical-pack.md`'s full Quality Control Checklist. At minimum: every item is checked against that list before being returned to the customer, by someone other than — or in addition to — whoever performed the sharpening, where practical for a single-operator business (a deliberate second look, even by the same person, at a different time, catches more than checking immediately after finishing the grind).

## Safety Workflow

- Sharp-edge handling: every item is handled by the spine/non-cutting section during intake, inspection, and packaging — never by the edge.
- Equipment safety: sharpening equipment is operated per its own manufacturer guidance; this SOP doesn't replace that.
- Processor blade handling specifically: see `docs/long-form-content/05-processor-blade-safety.md` — power disconnected before handling, blades transported in protective cases/wrapping.
- Damaged item handling: anything too damaged to sharpen safely is flagged and the customer contacted before any work proceeds, per the site's existing "if something needs extra work... I will contact you before proceeding" commitment.

## Cleaning Workflow

- Items are cleaned of sharpening residue (swarf, grinding debris) before packaging or hand-back — this is a distinct step from the sharpening itself, not assumed to happen automatically.
- Equipment (grinding surfaces, water reservoirs for water-cooled sharpening) is cleaned/maintained on a regular schedule to keep results consistent — exact frequency is an operational detail for the business owner to define based on volume.

## Blade Inspection Workflow

1. **At intake**: visible damage, rust, existing edge condition, and item type confirmed against what was booked/quoted.
2. **Before sharpening**: angle and condition assessed to determine restoration vs. standard sharpening approach (see `docs/technical-pack.md`'s workflow).
3. **After sharpening, before return**: full QC checklist applied (edge evenness, no burr, no heat damage, correct angle, and for scissors/clippers — tension and alignment).

## Packaging Workflow (Outbound, Mail-In Returns)

1. Item cleaned and dry.
2. Edge/tip protected (cardboard, cloth, or a dedicated guard) — same principle as customer-side packing in `docs/customer-education-pack/prepare-items.md`, applied in reverse.
3. Wrapped individually if multiple items are being returned together, so they don't contact each other in transit.
4. Packed into the return satchel/box provided (or an appropriate replacement if the customer's supplied packaging isn't suitable — flagged to the customer if so).
5. Tracking number generated and recorded before dispatch, then sent to the customer.

## Return Postage Workflow

- **Customer-provided satchel**: used as supplied; no extra charge.
- **Postage added to invoice**: cost calculated based on actual parcel weight/size at time of return packaging, added to the final total, and itemised so the customer can see it clearly (consistent with the site's "clear, upfront pricing" positioning).
- **Tracking**: always used for return postage, regardless of which payment method applies — this is already an established commitment on the live site ("Return postage is sent with tracking") and should never be silently downgraded to untracked postage to save cost.
